# GreenRoutine

**Small swaps, less waste, visible impact**

> #19 of 33 · Wave 2: Recurring keepers · Used: Daily/weekly · Medium · Revenue potential: Low–medium · Home & Household

## What it is

Track reusable habits (bottle, cup, bags), log food waste, get 'use it up' reminders, work through eco-swaps room by room, and never miss a recycling collection. Monthly impact summary.

## Why it's in the portfolio

A niche but passionate, loyal audience that shares values-led content. Cross-sells with Reset and PlateWeek.

## Who it's for

Eco-conscious households, zero-waste beginners, parents cutting plastic and food waste.

**The problem:** Good intentions fade without small, visible progress.

## Version one

- Reusable-item tracker
- Food-waste log and 'use it up' reminders
- Eco-swap checklist by room
- Recycling and collection-day reminders
- Monthly impact summary

**Example:** A household logs its reusable cups, gets nudged to use leftover veg, and sees its monthly waste fall.

## Later

- Household sharing
- Challenge packs (e.g. Plastic Free July)
- Impact report cards

| Stage | In GreenRoutine |
|---|---|
| Cue | Collection-day and 'use it up' reminders |
| Tiny win | Log a reuse or swap |
| Progress | Monthly impact |
| Share | Impact cards |
| Next step | Monthly challenge |
| Cadence | Daily/weekly |

## Animation and tone

**Playful.** Celebrations, confetti, cute characters, satisfying sounds and mild comic reactions. Nothing tragic.

- A small planet smiles more as the month's impact grows

**Line we don't cross:** No eco-guilt animations.

## AI

**None.** Not needed. Plain code does everything this app does.

## Watch-outs

Must never feel preachy. Impact numbers must be honest estimates with sources (no greenwashing). Collection days differ by council.
